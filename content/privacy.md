*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

## Information handled by this site

Decode the Stack is a static publication. It does not provide reader accounts,
registration forms, comments, advertising, or first-party analytics. The site
does not ask you to submit personal information.

The theme switch stores your light or dark preference in your browser's local
storage. That value stays in the browser and is not sent to the publication.

## Hosting and feed requests

Cloudflare Pages delivers the site and its public files.
Infrastructure providers may process connection and request information,
including IP addresses, for delivery, security, and service operation. See
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for
the provider's terms and practices.

Opening a publisher link sends a request to that publisher. Subscribing to an
RSS feed also means your feed reader will request the feed from this site.
Each service has its own privacy policy.

## Podcast audio

The podcast page and RSS feed are public. A podcast app may contact this site
to retrieve the feed and audio; the app's provider may collect information
under its own policy.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, open an issue in the
[Decode the Stack repository](https://github.com/ricmmartins/decode-the-stack/issues).
This notice may change as site features change; the date above identifies its
latest revision.
