The podcast is the audio edition of Decode the Stack's daily, source-linked
briefing. Anna, Maria, and Pedro discuss what the day's reporting says, connect
related developments, and explain the engineering context.

## Listen and subscribe

- **On this page:** The latest published episode appears above when its MP3 is
  available.
- **Podcast RSS:** [Subscribe in a podcast app](/podcast/feed.xml). The feed
  contains only episodes with a real, non-empty MP3 and matching show notes.
- **Written show notes:** Each episode links to the corresponding
  [source-linked edition](/posts/2026-11-02-daily/).
- **News RSS:** [Subscribe to written editions](/index.xml).

## The hosts

| Host | Style |
|---|---|
| **Anna** | The curious, energetic lead host who guides the conversation and transitions between stories. |
| **Maria** | The technical analyst who adds context, trade-offs, and thoughtful disagreement. |
| **Pedro** | The relaxed co-host who asks listener-focused questions and brings occasional light humor. |

## Format

| Detail | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Language | English |
| Hosts | Anna, Maria, and Pedro |
| Script length | Approximately 15 minutes |
| Voice generation | OpenAI text-to-speech (Marin, Sage, Ash); Edge TTS fallback |
| Topics | Software engineering, cloud, DevOps & SRE, AI/ML, and security |
| Schedule | Scheduled daily in UTC; a post is published when suitable stories and generation are available |

The script is generated from the day's source-linked synthesis. It adds
conversational transitions and context but does not replace the original
reporting; follow the source links in the show notes for full coverage.

The English RSS feed includes owner metadata for directory verification. A
podcast-directory listing link can be added once its official URL is available.
